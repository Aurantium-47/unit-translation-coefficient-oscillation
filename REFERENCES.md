# References and their roles

1. **Jenny Alvarez, Miguel Amadis and Leobardo Rosales.** *Unimodality and Log-Concavity of Polynomials*. Summer Institute in Mathematics for Undergraduates (SIMU), technical report, 2000. [Public transcription](https://www.academia.edu/121303677/Unimodality_and_Log_Concavity_of_Polynomials). The transcription displays 10 August 2000 and places the oscillation question in Section 3, printed p.99. It permits endpoint modes and already supplies a finite nonunimodal translate of a positive nonincreasing integer-coefficient polynomial. The institutional original/page image and complete resolution history have not been authenticated; the zero-difference convention in this note is explicit rather than attributed verbatim to that report.

2. **Bryan Ek.** *q-Binomials and related symmetric unimodal polynomials*. Author manuscript, 16 April 2018. [Rutgers author copy](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~bte14/Articles/Gnk/Gnk.pdf). Reference [AAR00] on p.32 corroborates the three authors, report title, SIMU and year. It is bibliographic evidence, not a resolution history of the oscillation question.

3. **George Boros and Victor H. Moll.** “A criterion for unimodality.” *Electronic Journal of Combinatorics* **6** (1999), R10. [DOI: 10.37236/1442](https://doi.org/10.37236/1442). Theorem 2.1 concerns nondecreasing coefficients and translation by one; it does not cover arbitrary unimodal or nonincreasing inputs.

4. **Jenny Alvarez, Miguel Amadis, George Boros, Dagan Karp, Victor H. Moll and Leobardo Rosales.** “An extension of a criterion for unimodality.” *Electronic Journal of Combinatorics* **8** (2001), R30. Published 19 September 2001. [DOI: 10.37236/1574](https://doi.org/10.37236/1574). Theorem 2.2 extends the nonnegative, nondecreasing input result to positive integer shifts. This six-author paper is distinct from the three-author 2000 report.

5. **Yi Wang and Yeong-Nan Yeh.** “Proof of a conjecture on unimodality.” *European Journal of Combinatorics* **26** (2005), 617–627. [Official author version, arXiv:0809.1586v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.1586v1), uploaded in 2008. The conjecture resolved there concerns nonnegative, nondecreasing inputs and arbitrary positive real shifts. It is not the unbounded-oscillation question.

These references provide context and attribution. The construction and its proof are included in full in the note. None of the references is used to infer first discovery or an exhaustive absence of prior work.
